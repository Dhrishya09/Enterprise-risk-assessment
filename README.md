# Enterprise-risk-assessment

### Project Meridian Energy (PME)

**Project Type:** Academic Cybersecurity Project  
**Domain:** Enterprise Security | Cybersecurity Risk Management | IT/OT Security

## Project Overview

This project involved conducting an enterprise-level cybersecurity risk assessment for Project Meridian Energy (PME), a simulated large-scale energy organisation undergoing digital transformation.

The organisation operates across corporate IT, operational technology (OT), cloud environments, renewable energy infrastructure and third-party networks. The assessment focused on identifying security weaknesses, evaluating potential business impacts and developing a risk-based security improvement strategy.

## Objectives

- Identify and prioritise enterprise cybersecurity risks.
- Assess risks using a 5×5 Likelihood × Impact matrix.
- Evaluate inherent and residual risk levels.
- Recommend appropriate risk treatment strategies.
- Establish risk ownership and governance structures.
- Develop measurable Key Risk Indicators (KRIs).
- Propose a phased cybersecurity improvement roadmap.

## Key Areas of Work

### 1. Enterprise Risk Identification
Identified eight cybersecurity risks involving:
- Identity and Privileged Access Management (IAM/PAM)
- Third-party and supplier remote access
- IT/OT network segmentation
- Ransomware and lateral movement
- Endpoint security
- Cloud and SaaS security
- Data protection
- Backup, recovery and business continuity

### 2. Qualitative Risk Assessment
Developed an enterprise risk register using a 5×5 Likelihood × Impact matrix to assess inherent and residual risk.

Each risk was evaluated based on its likelihood, potential business impact, existing security weaknesses and operational consequences.

### 3. Risk Treatment & Mitigation
Recommended security controls including:
- Phishing-resistant Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
- Privileged Access Management (PAM)
- Just-in-Time (JIT) access
- Network segmentation and restricted east-west traffic
- Vulnerability management
- Backup and recovery testing
- Improved security monitoring and logging

### 4. Governance & Continuous Monitoring
Established risk ownership across business service owners, cybersecurity leadership, IT/OT teams and vendor management.

Defined KRIs, periodic risk reviews and event-driven reassessment requirements.

### 5. Security Improvement Roadmap
Prioritised cybersecurity policies and proposed immediate actions within the first 90 days, followed by longer-term security improvements within 12 months.

## Key Findings

The highest-priority identified risk was unauthorised supplier access to engineering and OT systems.

| Risk ID | Risk | Inherent Risk | Residual Risk |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| R1 | Unauthorised supplier access to engineering/OT | 25 – Critical | 6 – Moderate |
| R2 | Ransomware and lateral movement across acquired networks | 20 – Critical | 6 – Moderate |
| R3 | Privileged identity compromise | 20 – Critical | 12 – High |
